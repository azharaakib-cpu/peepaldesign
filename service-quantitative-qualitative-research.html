Quantitative and qualitative research

We help teams understand their users — through numbers and through conversations. One tells you what's happening. The other tells you why.

Enquire Start a Project

Two ways of understanding users

Most projects need both. One counts what is happening across a crowd; the other sits with a few people and asks why.

Quantitative

“How many, how often?”

Collects and analyses numerical data — survey responses, analytics events, usability metrics — to find patterns across a sample large enough to trust.

Qualitative

“Why?”

Talks to real users — through interviews, observation, and usability sessions — to understand the motivations and context behind their behaviour.

Quantitative vs. qualitative

Two different shapes of strength. Quant spreads wide and validates; qual goes deep and explains.

Research pays for itself in decisions you don’t have to reverse

Four ways combining both methods de-risks a decision that one alone couldn't.

Data-driven decisions, not defended opinions. Replace assumptions with measured insights backed by real user data — from numbers and from direct observation — so a roadmap debate ends with a number on the table, not just the loudest voice in the room.

The full picture, not half of it. Numbers show you where the problem is; conversations show you why it’s happening. Together, they point to a fix — either one alone tells you there's a problem, not what to do about it.

Risk reduction before you build. Validate ideas before you build them, and understand user reasoning before you commit to a direction, so the expensive version of the mistake never makes it to production.

Confidence to act, either way. Whether it’s a stakeholder who wants numbers or a design decision that needs context, you have evidence either way — nobody's left arguing from a hunch.
