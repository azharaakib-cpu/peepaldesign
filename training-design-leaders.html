Curriculum

Training agenda

What you will learn. Seven modules run September to December — open one to see the question it answers.

How do you collect, organise and use customer knowledge?

  • Introduction to design maturity.
  • How to combine research results from different sources.
  • How to construct ready-made method sets for various project situations.
  • How to improve the effectiveness and impact of research by developing ResearchOps.
  • How to build communication rules between UX/CX/SD research and the business.

4 hours live sessions · 4 hours self-paced content

How do you improve service and product design processes?

  • How to formulate an effective design brief.
  • How to build and validate concepts of services, and of changes to services.
  • How to use prototypes appropriately.
  • How to build a set of resources, practices and repositories for better design management — DesignOps.
  • How to properly involve different design roles and stakeholders at the right stages of a project.

4 hours live sessions · 3 hours self-paced content

How do you match skills and responsibilities across a team, and foster cooperation?

  • How to manage roles, skills and cooperation rules.
  • How to plan a system of career paths for designers, researchers and other UX specialists.
  • How to build motivation, responsibility and alignment among designers.
  • Whether the way UX/CX/SD teams work can build employee experience across the whole organisation.
  • How to improve practices around cooperation and feedback.

4 hours live sessions · 3 hours self-paced content

How do you make sure your organisation’s culture supports design?

  • What tactics to use to convince stakeholders.
  • What design transformation means alongside the organisation’s other transformations — Agile, Lean, digital.
  • How to inspire and strengthen customer-centric initiatives among employees.
  • How to build commitment step by step, from the board and senior managers down.

4 hours live sessions · 3 hours self-paced content

How do you estimate and measure the value of design?

  • How to describe the impact our work will have on the lives of our audiences.
  • How to define indicators of success.
  • How to choose business metrics that help observe and prove change.
  • What tools to implement to measure user experience more effectively.

4 hours live sessions · 3 hours self-paced content

How do you include design in the company’s strategy?

  • How to help integrate UX/CX/SD goals into the company’s strategy, vision and mission.
  • How to systematically help implement the UX/CX/SD strategy.
  • What to propose for organisational structure and the relationships between teams.

4 hours live sessions · 3 hours self-paced content

How do you combine the six dimensions of design maturity into one system?

  • The role and career path of a design leader.
  • Key leadership skills.
  • Trends and predictions.
  • Course summary.

3 hours live sessions

Audience

Participant profile

The programme is written for people already leading, or about to.

This programme is for you if
  • You are a UX, CX or service design team leader.
  • You are just starting a leadership role, or your team is growing.
  • You want to improve your team’s dynamics and its relationships with other teams.
  • You want to attract and retain the most talented designers, researchers and strategists.
  • You are looking for ways to increase the maturity of design in your organisation.
  • You have completed the UX-PM Certification and are looking for what comes next.
Outcomes

What you gain by taking part

The programme is aimed at three levels at once — you, the team you run, and the organisation around it.

Your competencies

  • You develop the UX/CX/SD area in your organisation, and your own career, deliberately.
  • You build the team and the practices so that UX delivers business results and a good working culture.
  • You test your daily challenges against the view of other leaders.
  • You stay in touch with trainers and participants, supporting each other in building design maturity.

Your team

  • You create a working environment that attracts and keeps specialists and experts.
  • You improve collaboration and feedback practices.
  • You build motivation, accountability and alignment among designers.

Your organisation

  • You set a long-term strategy for UX/CX/SD transformation.
  • You analyse your organisation’s design maturity across six dimensions, then plan to raise it.
  • You order your organisation’s UX/CX/SD goals with knowledge gained from experts.
  • Seeing the perspective of people in other positions, you get a broader picture of what your company has built — and improve it.
