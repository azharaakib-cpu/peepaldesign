ResearchOps Discover and understand the principles

  • Support UX researchers doing research, increasing the efficiency and impact of research.
  • Learn about the key building blocks of ResearchOps, and turn knowledge into action.
  • Once you have finished the training, you have the means to kick off Research Operations practices in your organization.

Overview

ResearchOps in a nutshell

The shape of the training: how long it runs, who is in the room, and what you leave with.

Two half-days

Four modules across two mornings.

Small groups

Individual, or a private group.

Practical

Exercises and concrete examples throughout.

Networking

Specialists from many sectors in the room.

Certificate

Participation certificate sponsored by UXalliance.

Curriculum

Training agenda

What you will learn. Two half-days, four modules — open one to see what it covers.

  • Why customer centricity is one of the most strategic challenges of organizations today
  • The history of Ops
  • What is ResearchOps?
  • The 6 ResearchOps components
  • How ResearchOps can support teams
  • Concrete examples of successful implementation of Ops
  • ResearchOps as a service
  • How to start with your Ops service
  • How to measure and show the value of Ops
  • Identify who are your future ResearchOps users
  • Mapping the current state of the Research practice in your organization
Audience

Participant profile

Who the course is written for, and the grounding it assumes before you arrive.

Position
  • Anyone interested in research operations (PO/PM, lead designer, designOps, designer, researcher).
  • Product teams, and others, who want to scale up the research.
Experience
  • An understanding of the fundamentals of User Research projects, whether or not you have conducted research yourself.
Context

Challenges

The gaps this training is built around, in organizations already doing research.

Processes lack efficiency

More organizations have a UX research approach, but recruitment, governance and knowledge transfer drag.

No tools, no methods

Most ResearchOps positions in organizations do not have them.

Spend less, achieve more

There is a need for better processes.

Faculty

Experienced global trainers

The course is taught by UXalliance practitioners who run research and service design work of their own. Open a card to read a background.

Joelle founded the UX and service design agency Yu Centrik in Montreal in 2003; Yu Centrik is the exclusive Canadian partner of UXalliance. From the start she has been involved in developing and teaching the UX-PM Certification Program, and she teaches all three levels as well as Design Thinking and Service Design. A pioneer in the adoption of UX and service design in Canada, she began her career as a designer and has spent twenty years as a UX researcher and designer on digital projects with well-known international companies.

Domitille helps teams design and implement the experience of their digital products and services. A multidisciplinary background in marketing and CRM, for advertisers and agencies alike, lets her understand her clients’ experience design issues across path, data and CX. She works with airline and water utility services, and manages the deployment of a ResearchOps system in a bank.
