How we hire

The application process

Designed to be transparent and candidate-friendly. Not just an evaluation — a two-way conversation.

Step 01

Getting to know you

We start with your story, skills and ambitions. This first step helps us understand what motivates you, and where your strengths can create the most impact.

No task, no whiteboard. A conversation about the work you have done, the work you want to do next, and whether the two point in the same direction as the roles we have open.

Step 02

Take-home task

A short, realistic problem drawn from the kind of work we actually do. Not a trick question, and not a free deliverable for a live client project.

We tell you upfront roughly how long it should take, and you fit it around your week rather than a countdown. What we read is how you frame the problem and what you chose to leave out — not only the artefact at the end.

Step 03

A chat

A conversation with the people you would actually sit beside, not a panel you have never met.

We walk through your task together and talk about how you think when the brief is unclear. Half the time belongs to your questions — about the team, the projects, or anything you need in order to decide about us.

Step 04

Valuable feedback

Whatever the outcome, you hear back with something you can use.

We tell you what stood out, where we saw gaps, and how we reached the decision. Interviewing takes real effort, and being left without a straight answer is the part of hiring candidates tell us they find hardest.
