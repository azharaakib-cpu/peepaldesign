About UX-PM

Peepal offers the international UX-PM certification in India for senior practitioners responsible for managing digital projects — certified by UXalliance and recognised internationally. Worldwide, the certification runs in 19 countries and 12 languages.

The programme runs in three cumulative levels. Together they carry a team from customer awareness, through disciplined execution inside real delivery cycles, to leading UX across an organisation and measuring what it changes.

  1. Level 1 Adopting UX key concepts, lightweight methods, and becoming a UX ambassador inside your team.
  2. Level 2 Executing UX planning, monitoring and managing UX activities across the project lifecycle, including Agile delivery.
  3. Level 3 Leading UX maturity models, service thinking, co-creation, and Key Experience Indicators (KEIs).

Benefits

For organisations

  • Reframe UX as an investment with measurable impact on ROI.
  • Build internal capacity, improve delivery quality, and lift project success rates.
  • Develop a UX maturity lens to steer the organisation toward customer-centricity.

For project & product managers

  • Adopt a customer-centred mindset when shaping products and services.
  • Integrate UX methods inside Agile development cycles.
  • Use a practical toolbox to raise product quality and process efficiency.
The framework

Six dimensions of design maturity

The certification is built on the same UXalliance Design Maturity Model used to score how customer-centric an organisation really is.

Strategy

Is design-centricity sponsored by top management, with a UX strategy shared across departments and tied to measurable business outcomes?

Design

Does customer-centric design pervade every project, driven by data and regular prototyping rather than guesswork?

People

Do cross-functional teams with the right mix of skills tackle design problems from a shared customer-centric mindset?

Culture

Does the organisation actively support continuous learning, empathy, and innovation around the customer experience?

Metrics

Are Key Experience Indicators (KEIs) tracked alongside traditional KPIs to quantify the business impact of design?

Research

Are customer insights collected across the whole journey, through a well-structured process, and embedded into every product decision?
