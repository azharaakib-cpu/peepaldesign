UX team augmentation

Senior UX designers and researchers who join your team directly — your tools, your process, your standards — without the hiring cycle.

Add UX talent

Inside your team, not a handoff away

An outsourced project versus team augmentation — and why only one actually joins your standups.

An outsourced project

“We'll deliver it and hand it back.”

Handled separately, on someone else's process, returned as a deliverable. Useful for a defined package — but it lives outside your team's day-to-day.

Team augmentation

“We join your standups on Monday.”

Experienced designers and researchers work inside your existing team — your tools, sprints, and processes — adding capacity or expertise without the cost of a full-time hire, and scaling up or down as needs change.

Capacity when you need it, without the hire

Four reasons this beats a full-time hire when the need is real but not permanent.

Faster than hiring. Skip the recruiting cycle and add experienced UX capacity in days, not months — no job posting, no interview loop.

Fills specific gaps. Bring in exactly the expertise you're missing — research, interaction design, a design system specialist — without upskilling your team from scratch.

Flexible by design. Scale support up during a launch push and down once the peak work is done, with no fixed long-term commitment either way.

You stay in control. Strategy and product direction stay with your team; augmented talent handles the execution, not the roadmap.
